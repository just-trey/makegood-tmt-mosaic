Mosaic

for MakeGood TMT · SVG → multicolor recess geometry

The mark is the real MakeGood logo asset (assets/makegood-logo.png), 34px tall in the header, followed by a hairline divider and the "Mosaic" wordmark in Outfit 18px/600. There is no conic-gradient swatch — an earlier version of this page showed one, and the app has never had it.

The app renders exactly one colour blend: the 8px .accent-stripe above, a linear-gradient(90deg, var(--accent), var(--accent-2)) spanning the full window width. It is drawn twice, once above the header and once along the bottom of the window, from the same rule. Both stops are tokens, so the stripe cannot drift from the palette the rest of the app is built on.
