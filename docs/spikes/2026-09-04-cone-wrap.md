# Spike: cone-singularity unwrap of the chair's left+back+right band

**No-go: mirror plus per-zone is the ceiling.** Boundary First Flattening (BFF, Sawhney & Crane,
prebuilt v1.6) never produced a usable chart of the band at any cone count, and the reason sits
upstream of cones: the band the bake works on is not a surface. As the bake sees it (parts welded
at coincident vertices, stitched at 0.6 mm) it is vertex-glued, with 2,075 four-way edges and 792
boundary loops, and BFF splits it into more than 1,336 components. Sewn into a surface it has
genus 6, so any flattening has to cut it. Fattened and unioned into a genus-0 surface with
Manifold, its retriangulation defeats BFF and the bake's own LSCM alike. The one number the plan
wanted, BFF's stretch and fold on a clean band, was not reached; everything measured on the way
points the same direction as the three earlier dead ends.

**Spike, not a plan. Nothing here is committed to and the prototype must not merge.** The
prototype was `scripts/spike/` (18 node scripts) on the throwaway branch `spike/cone-wrap`, plus
BFF's Windows binary under `/mnt/c/bff-spike/`. Only this write-up and the `docs/roadmap.md` edit
are on the branch; **the code is not in the tree**, which is the intended end state.

Run 2026-09-04 against commit `89b474d` (main after #288), WSL2 Ubuntu 26.04 on the RTX 2060 box,
node v24.18.0, `manifold-3d` 3.5.1, BFF v1.6 (release 2023-05-16). Every number below re-derives
from the commands quoted next to it, all run from the repo root.

## Read first

[pipeline.md](../pipeline.md), "Artwork can't cross between zones", records the three measured
dead ends: a cylindrical band (69.6% of the surface inside `DISTORTION_WARN`), one merged LSCM
zone (max stretch 1.54, 4.85% of the chart covered twice), cross-chart registration (10 shared
vertices on 22 mm of a 500 mm boundary). This spike does not re-derive them; it reproduces the
merged-LSCM number as a calibration and then asks the one remaining question, whether cone
singularities change it.

## What blocked BFF from source, and what ran instead

- WSL has no C++ toolchain: `cmake`, `gcc`, `g++`, `cc`, `clang`, `make`, `ninja` are all absent
  (`command -v`), `libsuitesparse-dev` is not installed (`apt-cache policy`), and `sudo -n true`
  wants a password. Building from source was out; per the brief nothing was ported.
- The v1.6 release carries `windows-v1.6.zip` (18.3 MB: `bff-command-line.exe` plus SuiteSparse and
  OpenBLAS DLLs, `gh release view v1.6 -R GeometryCollective/boundary-first-flattening`). WSL
  interop runs it directly; it must be invoked with a cwd under `/mnt/c` (a UNC cwd is refused).
  It flattens its own `face.obj` (17,157 vertices) in 0.4 s and the band in under 2 s.
- Its usage line is
  `bff-command-line.exe IN OUT [--nCones=N] [--flattenToDisk] [--mapToSphere] [--normalizeUVs] [--writeOnlyUVs] [--scaling=S]`.
  **There is no way to hand it a cone list**, so the "cones forced into dead regions" run in the
  brief is unreachable from the CLI. Cone placement below is BFF's own.

## 1. The band as the bake sees it

`npx vite-node scripts/spike/dump-band.mjs scripts/spike/out` — the bake's `weldParts` at the
config's `seamWeldTolMm` 0.6, then the union of the shipped sidecar's per-part `tris` for
`left`, `back`, `right`, written as one OBJ.

| Zone  | Tris   | 3D area (cm²) | Sidecar stretch max / mean |
| ----- | ------ | ------------- | -------------------------- |
| left  | 10,093 | 1244.8        | 1.2242 / 1.0142            |
| back  | 12,952 | 962.0         | 1.1341 / 1.0057            |
| right | 12,275 | 1244.3        | 1.2259 / 1.0159            |
| sum   | 35,320 | **3451.1**    |                            |

- 20,825 vertices, 35,320 triangles, **one connected component**: the three zones do touch after
  welding. But `back`–`left` share 8 edges and `back`–`right` 8, and across every printed seam
  the parts share only 1–13 edges (`glue-diag.mjs`). Everything else is T-junctions: 792 boundary
  loops, 8,420 boundary edges, 225 pinch vertices.
- 2,075 edges carry four triangles, every one of them two faces from each of two parts: where a
  part's skin lies on its neighbour's (wing on wheel mount, handle on storage box, seat-back top on
  seat-back bottom) the zone claims both skins. 3,232 faces, 6.2 cm² (`nm-diag.mjs`).
- So the shipped charts are held together at vertices, not along edges, and LSCM copes because it
  only needs a connected linear system. A halfedge flattener does not. **BFF on this file splits
  it into over 1,336 components** (`Setting nCones to 8 for component 1336`).

## 2. Calibration: the measurement reproduces the sidecar and the dead end

`scripts/spike/measure.mjs` imports the bake's own `orientChart` for stretch and `flipped`, and
adds a 0.5 mm raster of the UV triangles that reports the share of covered pixels covered twice.
`npx vite-node scripts/spike/control.mjs`:

| Chart                              | Max stretch | Mean   | Flipped | Covered twice         |
| ---------------------------------- | ----------- | ------ | ------- | --------------------- |
| shipped `left`                     | 1.2242      | 1.0139 | 0       | 0.04% (0.4 cm²)       |
| shipped `back`                     | 1.1344      | 1.0057 | 0       | 0.16% (1.5 cm²)       |
| shipped `right`                    | 1.2259      | 1.0156 | 0       | 0.07% (0.8 cm²)       |
| merged LSCM, raw band (3 min 38 s) | 1.5401      | 1.0242 | 0       | **4.84%** (159.7 cm²) |

- Sidecar max reproduced to four decimals; mean within 0.0003 (edges on a cross-part seam are
  counted once per part chart here). The shipped zones' 0.04–0.16% is the doubled skin of §1.
- The merged LSCM gives 1.54 and 4.84% against the documented 1.54 / 4.85%, so the overlap raster
  measures the same thing the original spike measured.

## 3. Giving BFF a surface: two constructions, both fail differently

### 3a. Manifold union of the ten part solids

`npx vite-node scripts/spike/union-band.mjs <offsetMm> <selectMm>`: each packed part becomes a
Manifold solid, all ten are unioned, and a union triangle joins the band when its centroid lies
within `selectMm` of a §1 triangle and faces the same way (dot ≥ 0.7).

- Offset 0, select 0.6: parts that touch do not fuse, and the band comes back as 144 pieces with
  the largest holding 29.8% of the claim. Null result.
- Offset 0.4 (each part unioned with six axis-shifted copies of itself, closing the measured
  0.53 mm clearance gaps), select 1.0: **one component, 3386.1 cm² = 98.1% of the claim, genus 0**
  (V−E+F = −250 with 252 boundary loops), 72,157 triangles. The price is Manifold's
  retriangulation: 7,604 triangles with a corner under 0.5° against 2,881 in the CAD tessellation,
  plus 44 of zero area.
- The bake's own LSCM refuses this mesh outright: `LSCM solve did not converge in 327360
iterations (relative residual 5.68e-8) — the zone mesh is likely degenerate`
  (`lscm-union.mjs`). BFF accepts it and folds it (table in §5).
- Cleanup tried and abandoned: a link-condition edge collapse under 0.6 mm (24,655 collapses,
  3,720 slivers left) and under 1.5 mm (30,725 collapses, 156 zero-area caps left); a cap-flip
  pass that did not converge in 300 s. BFF folds every variant (§5).

### 3b. Zipping the CAD tessellation

`node scripts/spike/zip-band.mjs 0.6`: split a boundary edge at any other part's boundary vertex
within 0.6 mm of it (facing guard 0.3, the bake's `SEAM_NORMAL_DOT`), repeated to a fixed point;
where two skins coincide drop the smaller part's faces on the four-way edges; drop faces that
traverse an edge in the same direction as a neighbour; split pinch vertices; keep the largest
component.

- 1,109 splits in 15 rounds; 2.67 cm² of doubled skin and 0.73 cm² of fold-over dropped; the kept
  component is 34,569 faces, **3456.6 cm² = 100.2% of the claim**, 195 boundary loops, no
  non-manifold edges or vertices by the halfedge definition (`nmv-diag.mjs`).
- **Genus 6**: V−E+F = −205 with 195 loops. By zone: `left` 3, `right` 3, `back` 0. The handles
  are the coincident-skin patches: where a wing's flank continues under the wheel mount, or a
  handle post under the storage box, the two tessellations do not share edges inside the patch,
  so both skins survive and the rim of one closes a tube around the other. Deleting one skin
  wholesale disconnects it instead: the first zipper version did that and lost the whole left
  fender flank (397 cm²) as a separate component. Sewing the rim would mean splitting the other
  part's faces along a polyline, which is remeshing.
- BFF cuts a genus-6 surface before flattening it (183 cut edges, 373 cm, at `--nCones=0`) and the
  result is unusable (§5). The bake's LSCM on the same mesh: max 81.94, mean 1.5752, 38 flipped,
  3.58% covered twice. The 4.84% of §2 was never only the seams.

## 4. BFF on pieces it can flatten

`node scripts/spike/pieces.mjs <zone> <part> out.obj` writes one part's share of one zone, which
is a manifold subset of a closed solid. BFF `--nCones=0` against the bake's LSCM on the identical
file (`lscm-obj.mjs`):

| Piece                     | BFF max / mean / flipped | LSCM max / mean / flipped | Area-weighted angle error BFF / LSCM |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| `left` / wheel-mount-left | 2.849 / 1.027 / 2        | —                         | 0.118° / —                           |
| `left` / storage-left     | 105.9 / 1.337 / 92       | 1.1088 / 1.0014 / 0       | 0.243° / 0.007°                      |
| `back` / handle-left      | 27.47 / 1.286 / 171      | 1.1550 / 1.0225 / 0       | —                                    |

- BFF's chart of the storage flank is globally right (0.24° angle error, the CAD tessellation's
  own fans visible in `piece-left-storage-c0.png`) but folds locally: the 92 flipped triangles
  total 5.37 mm² and all sit at (170, 109–111, −522…−543), the screw-hole rims. Four cones make
  it worse (141 / 2.46 / 576 flipped, 21% covered twice).
- Wrong turns: filling every hole under 15, 100 and 1000 mm² first (`fill-holes.mjs`) leaves 114
  flipped either way; rescaling the file to metres changes nothing; `--flattenToDisk` gives 730
  flipped. The failure is BFF's conditioning on mixed-scale CAD triangles, not holes or units.
- So even where BFF gets a surface, it does not clear the shipped bars on a single part that the
  bake unwraps at 1.11. That is the strongest single result here: the prebuilt v1.6 is not a
  drop-in for the bake's LSCM on these meshes, before the band's topology is even reached.

## What this does and does not settle

- **Settled: a merged unwrap is not a bake-config change.** The plan's hope was that a shared
  parameterisation would be "just a bigger zone" through the existing bake. It cannot be, because
  the existing bake never builds a surface; it builds a vertex-glued complex that only LSCM
  tolerates. Any cone-based method (BFF, and OptCuts-style seam optimisation the same) needs the
  band remeshed into one genus-0 manifold of CAD-quality triangles first, and neither construction
  tried here gets there: the union has the topology and not the triangles, the zipper has the
  triangles and not the topology.
- **Settled: cones do not address what killed the merged LSCM.** On the zipped band, whose only
  change from §2 is edge gluing, LSCM's fold went from 4.84% to 3.58% and its max stretch from 1.54
  to 81.9. The fold is in the surface, not the seams, and the plan's curvature reading stands: net
  curvature ±0.47 turns per flank, Σ|K| 6.84 turns spread so thin that no handful of cones absorbs
  it, and only 6.6% of it under a wheel or cushion where a cone could hide.
- **Not reached: BFF's own stretch and fold on a clean band.** That needs a remesh (isotropic
  remeshing of the fattened union, or sewing the coincident-skin rims by face splitting). Nobody
  should attempt it without first checking §4: on a single storage flank the prebuilt binary
  leaves 92 folded triangles where the bake leaves none.
- **Not reached: cones placed in dead regions.** The CLI has no cone-list input; that run needs
  BFF's C++ API, which needs the toolchain this box lacks.
